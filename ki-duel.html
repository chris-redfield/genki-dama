Ki Duel

Pick your commands in secret, then fight in real time on the field below. He picked his too, and you can't see them.

Arrows / WASD
Move. While charging, turn to aim.
A: J / Space
Use your next command. Hold to charge ki blasts, release to fire. The gauge loops, so don't overcharge.
B: K / Shift
Hold to guard. It holds if your guard gauge is higher than his attack's power. Direction + B dodges, which burns ki.
Control Shot
Steer it with the arrows while it flies. A or B lets go.
Picking
Click or arrows + A to add, B to remove, Enter to fight.

Fight from the first second. Punch and blast as much as you like, as long as you have the ki for it. When you run dry, power up, but you stand still while you do.

Arrows / WASD
Move. While charging a blast, turn to aim.
A: J / Space / Z
Punch. Keep tapping to chain punch, punch, kick. Each hit costs a little ki.
B: K / Shift / X
Hold to guard. Hold a direction, then press B to dash (6 ki).
C: L / C
Ki blast. Tap for a quick shot, hold to charge a stronger one. From level 4, a full charge with 75 ki fires the Special Beam.
Power up: I / V
Hold to refill ki.
